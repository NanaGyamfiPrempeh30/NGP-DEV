## Context

Sika Track is a Telegram bot for bookkeeping. It is for informal businesses in Ghana, where the daily record is often a paper notebook.

## Problem

A trader will not open a spreadsheet at the end of a long day. Most already use a chat app. So the books should live in the chat.

## What I built

- A bot that reads plain messages such as "sold 50" or "spent 30 gas" and records them.
- Summaries of profit by day or by week, on request.
- A Flask app that receives messages from Telegram through a webhook.
- Supabase for the database and Render for hosting.
- A weekly encrypted backup.

## How it works

1. The owner sends a message to the bot.
2. Telegram posts it to the Flask app.
3. The app works out the amount and category and saves the entry.

A GitHub Action backs up the database every Sunday. The repo is public, so each backup is encrypted before it is stored. Backups are kept for 90 days.
