## Context

Abokobi Rural Bank's staff use this portal to log issues and to move requests through approvals that carry a signature. I work on it as the full-stack developer.

Built with Claude Code as my pair.

## Problem

The first version, in May 2026, logged issues. The bank then needed approvals with signatures, and a chain of approvals for inventory.

## What I built

- A signed approval workflow for staff requests.
- An inventory approval chain of 8 stages, with signatures and comments.

## How it works

The app runs on Node and Express, with SQLite for data. Docker Compose runs it with Caddy in front as the reverse proxy. Caddy serves the portal over HTTPS. Staff reach it over a private mesh VPN rather than the public internet.

## What broke and how I fixed it

Signature scans were too heavy. They now shrink in the browser before they are sent: 600 px wide JPEGs, typically 40 to 80 KB instead of 400 to 500 KB.
